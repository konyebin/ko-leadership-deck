180-Day Plan: Build and Elevate

Strengthen and modernize the program so associates build technical expertise, business acumen, and customer-facing confidence earlier. Create a scalable path from ramp to real customer contribution.

Assess the program, tighten readiness metrics, pilot improvements with measurable impact, then scale what works. Prioritize earlier customer and executive exposure alongside consultative skill-building. By day 180: clear milestones, standardized coaching, leadership alignment, and a 12-month roadmap.

  1. 1

    Days 1-30

    Assess and Establish Baselines

    Objective

    Understand program health, strengths and gaps, and define how success will be measured.

    Key Actions

    • Meet program leadership, hiring managers, associates, recent graduates, top SEs, and sales leaders
    • Review curriculum, onboarding, technical and business training, customer exposure, coaching, and current metrics
    • Identify skill gaps, associate pain points, hiring manager expectations, and readiness opportunities

    Deliverables

    • Current State Assessment: what works, gaps, what to preserve, where to improve
    • Success Metrics Framework: time to first customer engagement, time to independent interaction, associate readiness, manager satisfaction, graduation and placement
  2. 2

    Days 31-60

    Design Targeted Improvements

    Objective

    Design enhancements that accelerate customer-facing readiness and business acumen.

    Key Actions

    • Increase early customer exposure: discovery, executive conversations, adoption, renewals
    • Strengthen business acumen: customer outcomes, industry trends, executive priorities, financial drivers
    • Standardize coaching expectations across mentors, managers, and associates

    Deliverables

    • Associate Development Framework: milestones, competencies, expectations, success criteria
    • Coaching and Mentorship Structure: repeatable framework for associate growth and readiness
  3. 3

    Days 61-90

    Pilot and Measure

    Objective

    Validate enhancements and identify what delivers the greatest impact before scaling.

    Key Actions

    • Pilot updated onboarding, customer exposure, coaching approaches, and business-focused sessions
    • Measure engagement, readiness, confidence, manager feedback, and program outcomes

    Deliverables

    • Pilot Results Report: successful initiatives, areas to refine, adoption opportunities
    • Recommendations for Scale: prioritized by measurable impact and business alignment
  4. 4

    Days 91-180

    Scale, Optimize, and Institutionalize

    Objective

    Expand proven initiatives, standardize best practices, and position the program for long-term success.

    Key Actions

    • Scale successful enhancements; build playbook, coaching framework, and readiness milestones
    • Maintain alignment with program leadership, hiring managers, sales, and alumni
    • Assess evolving customer expectations, AI shifts, and SE role requirements; draft 12-month roadmap

    Deliverables

    • Program Playbook and Standardized Coaching Framework
    • Associate Development Milestones
    • Quarterly Program Review Process
    • 12-Month Strategic Roadmap
